Surf Lessons and Gear on the First Coast

First Coast Surf Co. helps beginners and experienced surfers enjoy Jacksonville's beaches. Book a lesson, rent a board or wetsuit, or check today's surf conditions before you paddle out.

What We Offer

  • Lessons

    Private and group lessons for every skill level, from first stand-up to your first green-wave ride.

  • Rentals

    Surfboards and wetsuits rented by the day or week, with sizes for kids and adults.

  • Surf Report

    Current conditions for six local breaks, from Ponte Vedra to Big Talbot Island.
